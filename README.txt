DREBIN — GOOGLE SITES REBUILD KIT

CONTENTS
- index.html: clickable, responsive reference preview of Home, About, and Product pages.
- assets/: extracted illustrations and image assets from the three supplied reference PDFs.

PREVIEW
1. Unzip this folder on your computer.
2. Open index.html in Chrome, Safari, or Edge. Keep the assets folder next to index.html.
3. Use the top navigation to switch between the three pages. The menu adapts for mobile screens.

IMPORTANT GOOGLE SITES NOTE
Google Sites cannot import an HTML/CSS website bundle as a native editable Google Sites template. Use this preview as the design reference while rebuilding the pages in Google Sites. Upload the images from assets/ to Google Drive, then insert them in Google Sites. Create three pages named Home, About, and Product page, and reproduce the sections in the same order. Use the built-in Google Sites theme/layout tools for text and spacing. For buttons, use Insert > Button and add your own destinations.

CUSTOMIZATION CHECKLIST
- Replace placeholder copy (the reference PDFs contain sample/Lorem-style text) with your real business copy.
- Replace example emails, phone numbers, team names, map, and product names as needed.
- Replace button alert placeholders with your own URLs or Google Forms.
- Google Fonts are loaded from the internet in the preview; Google Sites theme fonts may differ slightly.
- This package is a faithful visual/structural reference, not a native Google Sites import file. The exact original hosted template/theme cannot be reconstructed as a one-click import from PDFs alone.
